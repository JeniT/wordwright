The first thing my neighbour at plot 14 told me was that I had been too gentle. I had trimmed the blackcurrants the way I trim a sentence on a good day, a word here and a comma there, and they had thanked me by producing almost nothing.

“You have to take out a third,” she said, “and you have to take it from the middle.” I thought of every draft I had ever defended because it was mine and not because it was working.

Cutting is a way of paying attention

What I eventually understood is that pruning is not subtraction. It is a decision about where the plant will spend its energy next year. Take away the leader and something else has to lead; the question is whether you chose it.

A cut is a question you ask the plant. It answers by growing.

Editing is the same question asked of an argument. Which shoot is doing the work, and which is only taking the light?
