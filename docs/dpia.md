# Data protection impact assessment

Read this when you change how AI features send text, how reviewers or invitations work, what happens to a person's name when they leave, or you are asked why we decided a DPIA was needed. The facts it relies on are in [data-map.md](data-map.md). Written 26 October 2026 for #320; not legal advice, and the owner may want a lawyer to check it. Review it before launch, and again whenever one of the four areas below changes.

## Why one is needed

The ICO lists new technologies and the use of AI among the things that call for a DPIA, and Wordwright sends people's unpublished writing to a third-party AI. We have no special-category data as a purpose and no large-scale monitoring, but a writer's drafts can hold anything, so we assess rather than argue the threshold.

## The processing

Writers keep ideas, notes and drafts in publications they share with others. When a writer chooses, selected text goes to their own AI provider using their own key, and the answer comes back as suggestions. A writer can ask someone outside the publication to read a draft and comment (a reviewer), and can invite someone to join a publication. A person who leaves can keep or remove their name on work that stays behind. The purpose is to help people think about and finish their own writing ([responsible-ai.md](responsible-ai.md)). Volume is small (a private alpha), and the data is described in [data-map.md](data-map.md).

## Necessity and proportionality

- **We collect little:** name, email, username, a password hash or a Google identifier. No date of birth, location, phone number, IP address or device fingerprint is stored.
- **AI use is the writer's act each time**: no background calls, a button per call, nothing sent from the draft except what the feature needs (the table in the data map), comments and reviewers' words never sent.
- **No server-wide key and no training:** we hold no AI contract. The writer's provider terms apply, and the app tells them to use an API key (not a chat subscription), to check retention, and not to enter what they could not send a trusted contractor.
- **Nothing is published or sent onward by us**: AI output stays a suggestion the writer accepts or ignores.

## Outcome

None of the risks is high after the measures in place, so we do **not** need to consult the ICO before processing starts. This is conditional on closing the gaps that touch these risks before outside users arrive: the privacy notice and terms (#321) with the AI provider and role wording (gaps 2 and 6 in the data map), the age statement (5), and the deletion-after-restore step (8).
