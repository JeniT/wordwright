# Data map (UK data protection)

Read this when you change what the app stores, who it sends data to, how long it keeps it, or the wording of the privacy notice (#321). It is the record of processing (UK GDPR Article 30), the checklist for the other UK data-protection duties, and the list of gaps found. The assessment of the riskiest processing is in [dpia.md](dpia.md); what to do if personal data is lost is in [breach-procedure.md](breach-procedure.md).

Written 26 October 2026 for #320, from the code and the deploy docs. It is **not legal advice**: the owner may want a lawyer to check it before the public launch. Where a statement is a judgement rather than a fact in the code, it says so. Checked against the code at the commit that added this file; when you change a table, a retention period or a processor, change this file in the same commit.

## Who is who

| Role                                | Who                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| ----------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Controller**                      | The owner of Wordwright, who decides why and how account and usage data is processed. _Open: the legal person (an individual, or a company if one is set up) must be named in the privacy notice and on the ICO register. See gap 1._                                                                                                                                   |
| **Processors (ours)**               | UpCloud (hosting), Scaleway (sending email; backup storage), Gandi (DNS, and the `hello@` forwarder). Each acts only on our instructions.                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| **Independent controllers**         | Google, for the sign-in it runs (we receive the name, email and an account identifier). The writer's **AI provider** (Anthropic today), under the agreement the writer has made with it using their own key; see [byok.md](byok.md).                                                                                                                                    |
| **The writer, for what they write** | A writer decides what goes into their notes and drafts, and some of it is about other people. For that content we hold it on the writer's behalf, so in practice we act as their processor as well as controller of the account. _Judgement: the terms (#321) should say so and carry the Article 28 clauses; this is the first thing a lawyer should look at (gap 2)._ |
| **Data protection officer**         | Not required: we are not a public authority, and we do not monitor people at scale or process special-category data as our core activity. Reconsider if that changes.                                                                                                                                                                                                   |

There is no analytics, advertising, tracking or profiling, and no automated decision about a person: the only decision about an account (approval during the private alpha, #328) is made by the owner.

## What we hold

"Basis" is the lawful basis under Article 6. **Contract** means we need it to give the person the service they signed up for. **Legitimate interests** means security, preventing abuse and running the service safely; the person's interests were weighed and the data is minimal (see the note on each).

## What goes to a writer's AI provider

Only when the writer presses an AI button, and only with their own key. The text leaves our server for the provider they chose (Anthropic today) and is subject to that provider's terms; we keep no copy except the saved brainstorm conversation.

| Feature    | Sent                                                                                                                                     | Not sent                 |
| ---------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Brainstorm | The idea (title, notes, links), the publication description and the whole conversation so far, including other members' earlier messages | Draft text, comments     |
| Research   | The writer's description of the areas to look at, and the idea                                                                           | Drafts, comments         |
| Critique   | The draft and the publication description                                                                                                | Notes, threads, comments |
| Rephrase   | The draft, the selected wording, the aim, the description                                                                                | Notes, threads, comments |

The privacy advice shown beside the key field and on the Help page is in `ProviderPrivacyAdvice.vue` ([byok.md](byok.md)); the shared-thread notice is in `SharedThreadNotice.vue`. Reviewers' comments are never sent to any AI. The model's output is shown to the writer and kept only as a suggestion they accept or ignore ([responsible-ai.md](responsible-ai.md)).

## The rights, and whether they work

People can ask the owner at `hello@wordwright.io`; the answer is due within one month. Most can do it themselves in the app.

| Right                | How a person does it                                                                                                        | State                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| -------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Be informed          | A privacy notice, linked from sign-up                                                                                       | **Not yet written** (#321); this file is its source. The AI advice exists.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| Access / portability | _Account → Download your writing_: a zip of Markdown (`ExportService`)                                                      | **Partial.** It leaves out comments written by or about the person, reviews, brainstorm conversations, snapshots, and the account details (email, username, sign-up date, sessions, settings), so it does not answer an access request in full. The owner can compile the rest from the database (gap 3).                                              |
| Rectification        | _Account → Your details_: name, email (re-confirmed by link), username; writing is editable in the app                      | Works. A kept name on others' work follows renames only while the account exists.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| Erasure              | _Account → Delete account_ (password needed); immediate; the choice to keep a display name or show "a former member"        | Works for the live database ([account deletion](adr/accounts-and-security/account-deletion-semantics.md)): a test scans every table for a name that was not kept. **Backups hold the data for up to about 37 days.** Content a person wrote in a shared publication stays there, under the publication's continuing members, with the author unlinked. |
| Restrict, object     | Ask the owner. There is no direct marketing or profiling to object to; the legitimate-interests processing is security only | By email; the owner can approve, decline or delete the account (`/admin`, the admin CLI).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Withdraw AI use      | Remove the key under _Your AI_                                                                                              | Works.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Not be profiled      | —                                                                                                                           | Nothing to do: no profiling.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |

A reviewer-only account has no account pages, so it cannot export or delete itself in the app (it is routed only to its reviews). _Check, and if so file it (gap 5):_ a reviewer must be able to delete their account without an email to the owner.

## Gaps

Each is an issue: gap 1 #404; 2 and 6 #405; 3 #406; 4 #407; 5 #408; 7 #409; 8 #410; 9 and 10 #411.

1. **Name the controller and register with the ICO** (owner). Decide whether Wordwright runs as an individual or a company, register, and publish the number and a contact address. Blocks #321's privacy notice.
2. **Roles for the writer's content** (lawyer). Confirm we are processor for the writing and controller for the account, and put the Article 28 terms in the terms of use (#321).
3. **Subject access is only partly met by the export.** Add the account details, the person's comments, reviews and conversations to a fuller download (or write the owner's procedure for compiling it), and decide what to withhold where it holds other people's data.
4. **Scaleway's log retention** is unknown. Ask support or read the Specific Conditions, then record it here.
5. **Age statement and reviewer self-service.** Add the 18+ statement at sign-up and in the terms; check a reviewer-only account can delete itself.
6. **AI provider wording.** The privacy notice and terms must say the text is sent to the writer's provider outside the UK, and where the shared-thread rule changes whose provider sees whose words.
7. **File the processor agreements** (UpCloud, Scaleway, Gandi) and record when they were accepted.
8. **Deletions after a rollback or restore.** After `rollback.sh` or a restore, people who deleted their account since that backup reappear. Add a step to the runbook to repeat those deletions (keep a dated list of requests until the backups that predate them have expired), and rehearse it in #322.
9. **No retention rule for dormant accounts.** Only never-confirmed accounts are removed. Decide a period after which a long-unused account is warned and deleted, or state in the privacy notice that data is kept until the person deletes it.
10. **Taking back a kept name.** A person who chose to keep their display name cannot change their mind after deleting. Write the owner's short procedure for removing a kept name from `kept_name` columns on request.
