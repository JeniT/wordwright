# The idea landing page's wording (#232)

The idea's landing page follows the publication's: a page line and one `explain` per step, shown only in tutorial mode (route `idea` has `meta.tutorial`), and no explanation paragraphs on the page. What leaves the system stays beside each AI button as one line ("This will send … to your AI. It will not send your drafts."), in the shared `ResearchForm` so the landing page and the Research page say the same. The Refine invitation is a single button, **Refine with your AI**, which starts the conversation (the AI speaks first), and the AI is "Your AI" there, not "Coach". **New draft** is the one name for starting a draft, here and on the Drafts page. Counts are in the menu, not in the links. The Refine page itself still says "Start brainstorming" and "Coach"; that is #283.
