# The brainstorm is a writing coach, and the app owns when it stops (T43)

The default brainstorming prompt changed from a sceptical commissioning editor to a **writing coach** after the owner
found the editor too critical for working out what you think. What keeps the service honest is in the app-owned rules
(`prompts/brainstorm-rules.md`), which people can't remove. They say when to stop: ask the one question that matters
most and, once the writer can say what the idea claims, who it is for and why it matters, say so and stop asking (so a
prompt that tells the AI to keep going can't override it, though like all prompt-level behaviour it is eval-tested, not
guaranteed). The "keep asking questions that draw out more" line from T30 is gone, and the AI asks for the writer's own
title or angle before offering one. The same rules carry the persona-honesty, no-flattery and distress rules
([Persona, flattery and distress rules are the app's](persona-flattery-and-distress-rules-are-the-apps-not.md)). The
people-facing name for the AI is "Your AI", not "Coach" (the prompts still say writing coach: that is the AI's own
instruction, not what people read).
