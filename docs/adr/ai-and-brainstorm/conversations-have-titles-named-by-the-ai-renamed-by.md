# Conversations have titles: named by the AI, renamed by a person (#294)

Each conversation has a title (`idea_conversations.title`, with who gave it). The AI names it with a fifth tool, `name_conversation`, in its first reply: no extra request, up to 60 characters, applied at once with no accept step (a label, not a suggestion about the idea). Only the first valid call counts and only while there is no title, so the AI never overrides a person; its title is shown in the highlighter until someone renames it. A conversation the AI does not name shows its date and who began it in place of a title. Any member can rename (an edit icon beside the title) or delete it (an icon beside that, still asking first; the old text link under the thread is gone). The tool answers its call on the next turn like the others. `brainstorm-boundaries.test.ts` now allows five tools, and `docs/responsible-ai.md` says so. **Prompt changes (`prompts/brainstorm-rules.md`, new `prompts/tool-name-conversation.md`): the owner reviews them.**
