# Notes are suggested, not added (#295; supersedes T30)

T30 had the AI add the points a writer made to the idea's notes at once. It now **suggests** them (`suggest_note`, a proposal like the others), and everything it suggests appears in lists **above** its reply, outside the reply's box, so its words (usually a question) sit next to the reply box. Suggested notes, pages to read, and ideas or titles each have a list; a row has edit, add and ignore (an ignored one can be brought back); a page is laid out like a Research finding and reuses its row. Editing changes the suggestion itself (`PATCH …/proposals/:id`, only while it waits) so the edit is what is added and what other members see; adding checks duplicates and the 200-note limit again and leaves the suggestion waiting if it refuses. Points the idea already has are not suggested, and the AI is told why. The note allowance (6 per reply) is separate from the one for other suggestions, and the AI is told on its next turn whether each was added, ignored or is still waiting. Old threads keep their line for notes recorded the old way, and their calls to `add_note` still replay. The migration rebuilds `idea_proposals` to allow the new kind. **Prompt changes (`prompts/brainstorm-rules.md`, `prompts/brainstorm-default.md`, `prompts/tool-suggest-note.md`, which replaces `tool-add-note.md`): the owner reviews them.** Not done: the writer is not shown the suggestions' order of arrival while a reply streams (they appear once it is saved).
