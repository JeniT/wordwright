# The AI is told which notes are quotations (#293)

A note the writer took as a quotation (`quote`, from an article) used to reach the AI as a plain bullet, so it read as the writer's own point. The snapshot builder now takes the notes one by one and marks each quotation with its source and that it is not the writer's words, `[quotation from “Title”, not the writer’s own words] “text”` (no source once the item is removed), and the rules say to treat it as context: not the writer's view, not to be recorded as their point. There is no instruction to ask what they make of it; it is only context. Escaping is unchanged. The research brief got the same marking ([Quotations are marked in the research brief too](../research-and-feeds/quotations-are-marked-in-the-research-brief-too.md)). **`prompts/brainstorm-rules.md` changed: the owner reviews it.**
