# Persona, flattery and distress rules are the app's, not the writer's (#49)

"An AI, not a person", "no flattery, hold a fair criticism" and "in distress, drop the editor for a short warm reply"
live in `prompts/brainstorm-rules.md`, which always comes after the default prompt (principle 7 of
[responsible-ai.md](../../responsible-ai.md)), so no change to the persona can remove them. The distress rule is
deliberately narrow: acknowledge, point to people close to them or a professional or helpline, no probing, no notes
suggested, and no invitation to say more because other publication members can read the thread (principle 9). It is a
prompt-level behaviour and is not claimed to be reliable; the cases for it (`distress-gets-a-short-warm-reply`,
`no-unearned-praise`) are in the `current` group of the evals ([evals.md](../../evals.md)). Writing-to-think at
publication level (#25) is separate and not started.
