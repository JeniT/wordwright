# Reviewers get reviewer-only accounts, not profile membership or anonymous links

A person invited to review a draft gets an account that can reach only the reviews they were asked to do (a landing page listing them, then the snapshot and their own comments); every other route answers 404. It has no handle and no profile, and is upgraded to a full account when they are invited to a profile or choose to start writing. We rejected making reviewers profile members (too much access: the idea, notes, other drafts, other people's comments) and link-only access (anonymous authors, throttling and moderation, and no real authorship on comments). The cost is a second kind of account that every route must be checked against, so a test walks every route as a reviewer.

See #26 and `docs/decisions.md` → "Human review and draft editors".
