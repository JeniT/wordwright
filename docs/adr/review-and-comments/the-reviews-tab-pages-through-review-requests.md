# The Reviews tab pages through review requests (#302)

The tab shows one **review request** at a time (a snapshot, its reviewers and the invitations still waiting), the latest first, with Previous review and Next review as Refine pages its conversations. Its title is the date and time it was sent unless the editor or an owner names it (`draft_snapshots.name`, nullable, 80 characters; a blank name clears it; reviewers never see it). Progress is “1/2 complete · 3 comments”: who has finished, of those reading, finished or yet to answer (so an invitation not yet accepted counts as someone still to finish); closed ones don't count, and everyone closed reads “All closed”. Resolving comments is the Comments tab's job, so the tab no longer counts unresolved ones; the publication page's list uses the same line, and keeps its “Reviews finished, unresolved comments” flag, which is driven by the unresolved count. Every snapshot that has reviewers or a waiting invitation is summarised, each counting only its own reviews. Closing is confirmed in all three places (one reviewer, “Close these reviews”, “Close all reviews”), since a closed review can't be reopened and the line that used to say so is gone; “Close all reviews” only shows when other requests also have open reviews. `PATCH …/snapshots/:id` renames and `POST …/snapshots/:id/close` closes a request's open reviews; reviewers and invitations now carry their `snapshotId`.
