# Handover and review on the draft page: notices and dialogs (#296)

Handover and review were two closed disclosures above the title. They are now two things. **Notices**, under the notice bar, for what a handover is waiting for (an offer to you, a request to the editor: warn, with their buttons) and for what you have set going (your open offer or request: info, each with Withdraw), so nobody thinks Wordwright forgot what they did. **Buttons** in a quiet row beside “← All drafts” that open dialogs: Offer draft, Ask for this draft (a confirm, in case of an accidental press), Reassign draft, Send for review / Reviews, and Handover history (read-only, for every member). The opener hides while the thing it starts is already open, since the notice carries Withdraw. We rejected a “Draft settings” area (a new menu level that takes people off the writing page, and loses typed text unless saved) and left the notice bar's rule that a quiet page holds back info notices, narrowed to the bar's own notices. Saving before a snapshot is sent is #299's (autosave), which flushes first.
