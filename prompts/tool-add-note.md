Record one point the writer has made as a note on the idea being discussed. Use it as the conversation goes: each time the writer says something worth keeping (a claim, an example, a source, a reader, a question they want to answer), record it, one point to a note, in the writer's own words as far as you can. Do not paraphrase them into something tidier, and do not add points they did not make. The note is added to the idea straight away, so keep it short and do not record the same point twice.
