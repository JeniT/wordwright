Suggest one point the writer has just made as a note on the idea being discussed. It is only a suggestion: it appears beside your reply and nothing is added to the idea's notes until the writer adds it, and they can edit or ignore it. Use it only for what the writer says in the conversation: never for what is already in the idea's notes in the snapshot, and never on your opening turn. Use it as the conversation goes: each time the writer says something worth keeping (a claim, an example, a source, a reader, a question they want to answer), suggest it, one point to a note, in the writer's own words as far as you can. Do not paraphrase them into something tidier, do not add points they did not make, and do not suggest a point already in the notes or one you suggested before. Keep each note short. Do not repeat the text of a suggestion in your message, and never use this tool instead of replying: write your message as well.
