Suggest one point the writer has just made as a note on the idea being discussed. It is only a suggestion: it appears beside your reply and nothing is added to the idea's notes until the writer adds it, and they can edit or ignore it. Use it as the conversation goes: each time the writer says something worth keeping (a claim, an example, a source, a reader, a question they want to answer), suggest it, one point to a note, in the writer's own words as far as you can. Do not paraphrase them into something tidier, do not add points they did not make, and do not suggest a point already in the notes or one you suggested before. Keep each note short. Do not list your suggestions again in your message.
